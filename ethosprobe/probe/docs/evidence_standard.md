# Ethoculus Evidence Standard

Ethoculus uses an evidence ladder.

## Level 1 — Observation

A behavior occurred during an individual trial.

## Level 2 — Replicated Pattern

The behavior occurred repeatedly under substantially similar conditions.

## Level 3 — Association

A measurable relationship exists between an experimental condition and behavior.

## Level 4 — Controlled Treatment Effect

Changing the experimental variable while controlling other conditions
produces a reproducible change in behavior.

## Level 5 — Robust Treatment Effect

The effect survives replication, paraphrasing, alternative ordering,
additional controls, and other reasonable stress tests.

## Level 6 — Mechanism Hypothesis

The behavioral evidence supports a proposed explanation of internal behavior.

This remains a hypothesis.

## Level 7 — Mechanistic Evidence

Independent internal instrumentation or intervention supports the proposed mechanism.

Ethoculus should never represent evidence from a lower level as though
it establishes a higher level.
